Keep it looking good for longer.

01

Lift & shake

Take the mats out and shake off dry dust and crumbs before a deeper clean.

02

Rinse or wipe

Hose off mud and spills, or wipe the surface with a damp cloth and mild soap.

03

Let them dry completely so the anti-slip backing grips properly, then lay them back.
